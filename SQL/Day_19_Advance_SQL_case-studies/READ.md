# Day 19 - Advanced SQL Case Studies

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains real-world SQL business case studies designed to strengthen SQL problem-solving skills.

Students will apply concepts learned throughout the course to solve practical business scenarios using SQL.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

- Analyze business requirements
- Design SQL queries independently
- Use Joins effectively
- Apply Aggregate Functions
- Write Subqueries
- Use GROUP BY and HAVING
- Work with String, Numeric and Date Functions
- Solve interview-style SQL questions

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

- Case Study 1 – Multi-Table Joins
- Case Study 2 – Customer Analytics
- Case Study 3 – Sales Performance
- Case Study 4 – Product Insights
- Case Study 5 – Employee Performance
- Case Study 6 – Inventory Management
- Case Study 7 – Financial Reporting
- Case Study 8 – Business Dashboard Queries
