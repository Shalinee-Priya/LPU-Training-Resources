## Overview

Welcome to **Day 4** of the SQL learning series.

This module introduces **GROUP BY** and **HAVING**, allowing students to summarize data category-wise and solve real business reporting problems. Students will also learn **SQL Execution Order**, one of the most important interview concepts in SQL.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Understand SQL Execution Order
- Group records using `GROUP BY`
- Apply Aggregate Functions to groups
- Differentiate `WHERE` and `HAVING`
- Combine `WHERE`, `GROUP BY`, and `HAVING`
- Rank grouped results using `ORDER BY`
- Solve branch-wise and city-wise business analytics problems

- Why GROUP BY?

## Business Transition

Until Day 3, SQL answered questions about **individual records**.

Day 4 answers questions about **groups of records**.

Examples:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Highest CGPA | Highest average CGPA by branch |
| Total Fees | Total Fees of every branch |
| Count Students | Count Students city-wise |

This shift represents the beginning of **data analysis using SQL**.
